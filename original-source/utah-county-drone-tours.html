FAQ_INDEX // CLIENT QUESTIONS

Utah County drone tour FAQ.

Short answers before you request a project-specific quote.

What is a 360 drone tour?

It is an aerial experience where viewers can look in every direction. It may be a guided 360 video or a clickable virtual tour with connected scenes and hotspots.

Where are 360 drone tours available?

Heath is based in Provo and serves Utah County, including Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Vineyard, Springville, Spanish Fork, Saratoga Springs, and Eagle Mountain. Travel outside the county is available by quote.

How much does a 360 drone tour cost?

Basic 360 capture or a starter clickable-tour package begins at $350. Airspace, access, scene count, travel, editing, hosting, and final deliverables affect the quote.

Can the finished tour be shared with clients?

Yes. The finished experience can be delivered as a shareable link for a website, listing, presentation, email, or client review.

Can every property be flown?

Not automatically. Weather, airspace, people, access, site conditions, and the requested flight path are reviewed before the project is confirmed. A safer alternative can be proposed when a specific flight is not practical.
