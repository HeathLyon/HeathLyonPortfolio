# External components

MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.32 is dynamically loaded from jsDelivr; the Pose Landmarker Lite model is loaded from Google's official model storage when camera tracking is enabled.

- Package/source: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
- Pose documentation: https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker/web_js
- Package license: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@mediapipe/tasks-vision@0.10.32/README.md
- Model: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/pose_landmarker/pose_landmarker_lite/float16/1/pose_landmarker_lite.task
- YouTube iframe API: https://developers.google.com/youtube/iframe_api_reference

External packages/model weights are not bundled in this archive. Their terms apply separately.
