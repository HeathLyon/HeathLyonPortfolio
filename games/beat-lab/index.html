04 / Replay timing

Analyze a continuous section or the full video. Stop capture, then replay at 1× to compare saved onset flashes with the music.

Positive offset delays flashes. This prototype saves measured onset times, not choreography. It never records or exports audio/video.

05 / Debug console

Audio processing stays in this browser. Only derived timing and diagnostics are exported when you click Export. YouTube receives normal player requests. Refresh clears the session.
